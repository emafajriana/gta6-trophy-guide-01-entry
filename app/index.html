The road to the Platinum

55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. The list is built from the official descriptions; the timings and method come from PowerPyx's guide, cited in the Sources tab.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours · 12 story trophies

    Finish the five chapters and the story missions. The 12 story trophies pop as you go and cannot be missed. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude (see Missables), scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you meet them — both count toward 100% later. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter: there are 15 manual slots, no mission replay and no New Game+.

  2. Step 2 — Side characters & strangers 6 side trophies + 1 Silver

    Finish the questlines for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret as they appear, and complete 8 Strangers & Freaks strands. Every one of these stays available after the story, so nothing here is missable — but do Yago's street-racing strand when it shows up, because one of its races is time-sensitive.

  3. Step 3 — Activities, collectibles & crime the long grind

    Work the counters: 250 stunts, gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 Top Fun drone-race locations, 20 race wins, 3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 5 bank vaults, 39 illegal gang operations, 52 areas explored. Most of these feed the 100% checklist, so do them before the final clean-up.

  4. Step 4 — 100% completion, then the Platinum Gold: A Long Road, then the Platinum

    After the story, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is forgiving — roughly 60% of the game's content — and nothing on it is missable. Finish any 100 optional missions, scan 200 of 277 vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals, complete 300 stunts and any 70 of 80 challenges, and the Gold pops. The Platinum follows the moment the last trophy does.

Three rules that protect the Platinum

  • Cheats switch trophies off — and switch saving off too. Do not use a cheat until the Platinum has popped.
  • No mission replay. A story moment you walk past is gone unless you kept a manual save. Save at the start and end of every chapter and once more before the final mission.
  • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. It is the only trophy with a deadline in the whole list — everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Missable trophies

Built from the official descriptions and checked against PowerPyx, GTABase and OutputLag: exactly one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Not missable — but watch these

  • Strangers & Freaks — Yago's “Race Against Yago” in Chapter 3 carries an hourglass icon: it is time-sensitive. Do his street-racing strand when it appears. The guides disagree on whether skipping it can actually lock the trophy, so do not leave it to chance.
  • Optional missions disappear. Many optional missions (black icons with pink letters) vanish after a while, and some quest givers go quiet for a stretch of the story. PowerPyx reports this does not affect any trophy: the trophy-related characters all return, and there are far more than the 100 optional missions the 100% checklist needs.
  • The final mission. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save from just before it — not because a trophy depends on it, but because you cannot replay the ending otherwise.
  • Your criminal profile. The “Irredeemable” profile is permanent and makes the police far more aggressive. It does not block any trophy, but avoid racking up excessive violence if you can.

Sources & method

Every name, description and grade in this guide was checked on 19 November 2026 against Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on all 55. The guides are written in our own words from the official descriptions plus the guides cited below — their text is never copied.

What we verified, and what we did not

  • Verified — 55 of 55. Name, official description and grade for every trophy, against Exophase and PSNProfiles. Grade split: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. 28 trophies are hidden (12 story, 6 side-character, 10 secret). PSN progress targets are from PSNProfiles.
  • Not yet confirmed — 6 trophies. No published guide had confirmed a method for In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin when this guide was built, and the exact animal for Fair Game was still being confirmed. Their guides say so plainly and record the leading guess as a guess, not a fact. Use the “Unconfirmed only” filter to see them.
  • Possibly missable — 1 trophy. Positive Mental Attitude: whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is required is still unconfirmed, so the guide tells you to do it anyway.
