The road to the Platinum, in four stages

55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 of them hidden. Roughly 200 hours, difficulty about 4 out of 10, one playthrough, and no online trophies anywhere in the list. Each stage below names the trophies it clears — the order is built from the official descriptions, and every trophy's own steps are cited in its guide.

Three rules that protect the Platinum

  • Never touch a cheat before the Platinum. Cheats switch trophies off — and saving with them. The ammo cheats the shooting-range golds unlock stay locked away until the Platinum has popped.
  • Save like there is no replay — there is none. No mission replay and no New Game+: keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter, and one more before the final mission.
  • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. The balcony session at Jason's house is the only deadline in the entire list. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Missable trophies

Checked against the official descriptions and the published guides: exactly one trophy in the whole list can be missed, and even that one is only possibly missable — the safe play costs two minutes in Chapter 1. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

Not missable — but watch these

  • Yago's race is time-sensitive. 'Race Against Yago', inside the Strangers & Freaks strands in Chapter 3, carries an hourglass icon. The guides disagree on whether skipping it can lock the strand's trophy, so run it when it appears rather than testing the question.
  • Optional missions come and go. Many optional missions (black icons, pink letters) vanish after a while, and some quest givers go quiet for a stretch of the story. The trophy-bearing characters all return, and the 100% checklist needs only 100 of far more optional missions than that — but do not bank a strand you have already started.
  • The ending cannot be replayed. No trophy hangs on the final mission beyond finishing it, but with no mission replay, a manual save from just before it is the only way to see the ending twice.
  • One profile is permanent. The 'Irredeemable' criminal profile never washes off and makes the police far more aggressive. It blocks no trophy; it just taxes everything after it. See Actions Speak Louder.

What was verified, and what was not

  • Verified — 55 of 55. Name, official description and grade for every trophy, cross-checked between Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on all 55; the hidden split (28 hidden: 12 story, 6 side-character, 10 secret) cross-checked against AlcastHQ. PSN progress targets are PSNProfiles' figures.
  • Unconfirmed — 6 trophies. No published guide had confirmed a method for Fair Game's exact animal, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There or Ten Pin when this fieldbook was written. Their guides say so in plain words and label the leading idea as a guess. Filter the checklist by 'Unconfirmed only' to see them.
  • Possibly missable — 1 trophy. Positive Mental Attitude: whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is required is unconfirmed, so the guide tells you to do it anyway and keep the save.
